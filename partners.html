Quick answers

Who can become a TECKONO partner?

Distributors, online retailers and marketplace sellers, consumer electronics stores, corporate buyers, B2B procurement companies and international trading companies. If your business operates in electronics, appliances, gaming or accessories, you are eligible to join our partner network.

How do we start?

Contact us with your company details, or register through the Partner Portal, and receive your dedicated account manager. You get pricing and stock availability, place your first trial order and we establish long-term cooperation with prioritized conditions.

Where do you ship from?

From our logistics hubs in Hong Kong, Shenzhen (China) and Klecany (Czech Republic, EU). We deliver worldwide by air, ocean, truck, rail and express courier.

What are the partner requirements?

A valid company registration, active VAT/EORI for EU partners, the ability to process B2B transactions and a basic order volume depending on the product category.

Which products do you distribute?

Premium consumer electronics from 40+ brands: household and kitchen appliances, gaming consoles, audio, home entertainment, power stations, drones, robotics and satellite internet equipment.
