TECKONO LIMITED operates worldwide, shipping to partners across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. To support global distribution, we work with professional logistics partners and warehouse facilities in several key regions. These strategic hubs allow us to route shipments efficiently to any destination worldwide.

Our logistics partners operate in several locations that serve as main export and distribution points:

  • Hong Kong: international gateway for global air and sea freight
  • Shenzhen, China: consolidation, inspection and high-volume export hub
  • European Union (Czech Republic): VAT-compliant EU distribution and fast regional delivery

These hubs do not limit our reach, they enable us to deliver quickly and efficiently to any country worldwide.

Quality assurance. All products come through verified suppliers and authorized channels. Before shipment we perform packaging checks, serial-number verification, accessory completeness checks and documentation control.

TECKONO LIMITED is a global distributor of premium consumer electronics, operating from our logistics hubs in Hong Kong, China, and the European Union. From these key locations we supply retailers, wholesalers, and e-commerce companies worldwide with high-demand products from leading international brands.

Working closely with certified suppliers and professional warehouse partners, we ensure consistent product availability, competitive B2B pricing, and secure international delivery to customers across multiple regions.

Our mission is to provide a transparent, reliable, and efficient sourcing experience, wherever our partners are located. Trust, speed, and long-term cooperation define the way we operate every day, supporting businesses globally from our hubs in Asia and Europe.

Quick answers

Who can become a TECKONO partner?

Distributors, online retailers and marketplace sellers, consumer electronics stores, corporate buyers, B2B procurement companies and international trading companies. If your business operates in electronics, appliances, gaming or accessories, you are eligible to join our partner network.

How do we start?

Contact us with your company details, or register through the Partner Portal, and receive your dedicated account manager. You get pricing and stock availability, place your first trial order and we establish long-term cooperation with prioritized conditions.

Where do you ship from?

From our logistics hubs in Hong Kong, Shenzhen (China) and Klecany (Czech Republic, EU). We deliver worldwide by air, ocean, truck, rail and express courier.

What are the partner requirements?

A valid company registration, active VAT/EORI for EU partners, the ability to process B2B transactions and a basic order volume depending on the product category.

Which products do you distribute?

Premium consumer electronics from 40+ brands: household and kitchen appliances, gaming consoles, audio, home entertainment, power stations, drones, robotics and satellite internet equipment.
